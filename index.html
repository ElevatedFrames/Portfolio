Approach

How I edit
for retention.

An edit isn't decoration on top of the footage. It's the reason someone stays past the first two seconds.

01

Hook-first pacing

The cut is built to earn the scroll-stop in the first two seconds, then hold it.

02

Captions built for sound-off

Most reels get watched muted first — the edit reads clearly with the sound down.

03

Clear, spoiler-aware cuts

Movie and anime edits give nothing away unless the review is meant to go deep; tech edits keep every point easy to follow.

04

Genre-matched grade & sound

Colour and sound design shift with the content — a clean tech explainer, a Marathi drama, a Hollywood blockbuster, and an anime arc don't get the same treatment.
